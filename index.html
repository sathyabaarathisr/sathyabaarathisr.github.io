About

I am a systems immunologist at The Jackson Laboratory for Genomic Medicine, where my research focuses on a fundamental question in aging biology: why do some older adults respond strongly to vaccines while others do not? Using bacterial pneumococcal (Prevnar, Pneumovax) and seasonal influenza vaccines as immunological models, I integrate serological response, single-cell transcriptomics and epigenomics, targeted inflammatory proteomics, and network biology to identify pre- and post-vaccination immune signatures that predict vaccine responsiveness.

During my Ph.D. at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, I worked on computational approaches to infection biology, spanning genome-scale network modeling, blood-based transcriptomic biomarkers for distinguishing viral from bacterial infections, RNA signatures for monitoring tuberculosis treatment response in longitudinal cohorts, and computational design of antimicrobial peptides against drug-resistant pathogens.
